# Character Relationships and Interactions

Supporting characters and the relationships that connect them to major characters in Asimov's Robot, Empire, and Foundation universe. Connections are grouped by story arc; direct encounters are distinguished from indirect plot links where useful.

## Foundation's Establishment

- **Lewis Pirenne**: Chairman of the Foundation's Board of Trustees. His faith in the Encyclopedists and resistance to political change put him at odds with Salvor Hardin. **Connections:** Salvor Hardin (political opponent); Foundation leadership (colleague). **Source:** *Foundation*.
- **Sef Sermak**: Leader of the Foundation's Actionist movement, which challenges Hardin's cautious approach to the neighboring kingdoms. **Connections:** Salvor Hardin (political opponent); Foundation (political rival). **Source:** *Foundation*.
- **Lord Dorwin**: Empire envoy whose visit to Terminus gives Hardin an opportunity to assess the Empire's declining influence. **Connections:** Salvor Hardin (diplomatic encounter); Lewis Pirenne (diplomatic encounter). **Source:** *Foundation*.
- **Anselm haut Rodric**: Anacreonian official who visits Terminus and confronts Hardin over the Foundation's political status and technology. **Connections:** Salvor Hardin (political encounter); Anacreon (representative). **Source:** *Foundation*.

## The General

- **Lathan Devers**: Independent trader captured during Bel Riose's campaign. He and Ducem Barr become allies and escape together. **Connections:** Ducem Barr (ally); Bel Riose (prisoner and adversary); Onum Barr (meets after the escape). **Source:** *Foundation and Empire*.
- **Ducem Barr**: Siwennian patrician and son of Onum Barr. He joins Devers in resisting Riose and escaping Imperial custody. **Connections:** Onum Barr (father); Lathan Devers (ally); Bel Riose (adversary). **Source:** *Foundation and Empire*.
- **Onum Barr**: Ducem's father, who shares his knowledge of Siwenna and Imperial rule with Ducem and Devers. His connection to Riose's campaign is indirect, through his son and Devers. **Connections:** Ducem Barr (father); Lathan Devers (encounter); Bel Riose (indirect plot connection). **Source:** *Foundation and Empire*.
- **Brodrig**: High-ranking Imperial courtier whose presence in Riose's campaign ties the general's military success to politics at Cleon's court. **Connections:** Bel Riose (campaign associate); Cleon II (court connection). **Source:** *Foundation and Empire*.
- **Cleon II**: Emperor who recalls Riose as the general's victories and growing influence make him a perceived political threat. **Connections:** Bel Riose (ruler and subject); Brodrig (court official). **Source:** *Foundation and Empire*.

## The Mule

- **Toran Darell**: Bayta Darell's husband and companion during the journey that brings them into conflict with the Mule. **Connections:** Bayta Darell (husband); Ebling Mis (traveling companion); Magnifico (traveling companion). **Source:** *Foundation and Empire*.
- **Ebling Mis**: Foundation psychologist who travels with Bayta and Toran and investigates the Second Foundation. **Connections:** Bayta Darell and Toran Darell (companions); The Mule (manipulates his search for the Second Foundation). **Source:** *Foundation and Empire*.
- **Magnifico Giganticus**: The Mule's assumed identity as a traveling entertainer. Bayta's sympathy and protection help conceal his real identity from the group. **Connections:** Bayta Darell (protector); Toran Darell and Ebling Mis (traveling companions); The Mule (same person). **Source:** *Foundation and Empire*.
- **Han Pritcher**: Foundation officer whose resistance to the Mule is overcome by the Mule's mental influence; he later serves as one of the Mule's generals. **Connections:** The Mule (converted follower and general); Foundation (former officer). **Source:** *Foundation and Empire*; *Second Foundation*.

## Second Foundation Search

- **Homir Munn**: Foundation librarian who takes Arkady Darell to Kalgan while investigating the Mule's former palace and the Second Foundation. **Connections:** Arkady Darell (travel companion); The Mule (his investigation is shaped by the Mule's influence). **Source:** *Second Foundation*.
- **Sura Novi**: Second Foundation agent who befriends Arkady and helps guide her through the events surrounding the search. **Connections:** Arkady Darell (ally and guide); Second Foundation (agent). **Source:** *Second Foundation*.

## Seldon Family

- **Raych Seldon**: Hari and Dors's adopted son, raised in Dahl. He becomes Hari's aide and later the father of Wanda and Bellis. **Connections:** Hari Seldon and Dors Venabili (adoptive parents); Wanda Seldon and Bellis Seldon (children). **Source:** *Prelude to Foundation*; *Forward the Foundation*.
